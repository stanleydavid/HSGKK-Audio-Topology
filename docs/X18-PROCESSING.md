## Threshold

Do not copy a fixed compressor threshold blindly. Threshold depends on preamp gain and performer level. Lower it until the target gain reduction occurs during normal use.

## Gates

Start with gates/expanders **OFF** on vocal and communication channels. If bleed requires control, prefer gentle expansion rather than hard gating that chops quiet speech or vocal tails.

## Processing philosophy

- AWL vocals: natural, moderate compression.
- WL/speaker wireless: controlled and intelligible.
- MD/PA: communication-first; prioritize intelligibility.
- Ch 8 Band: preserve the mix delivered by PreSonus; avoid unnecessary second-stage EQ/compression.
