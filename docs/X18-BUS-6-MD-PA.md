# X18 Bus 6 — MD / PA Return to PreSonus

Purpose: communication reference for musicians. This is **not** a vocal or band mix.

**Send mode:** Pre-fader.

| Ch | Source | Send |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Shure Wireless 1 — Speaker | OFF |
| 2 | Shure Wireless 2 — WL | OFF |
| 3 | AWL Vox 1 | OFF |
| 4 | AWL Vox 2 | OFF |
| 5 | AWL Vox 3 | OFF |
| 6 | MD Key/Bass/Gtr | 0 dB |
| 7 | PA Mic | -6 dB |
| 8 | PreSonus Band | **OFF — mandatory** |
| 9 | Additional Wireless | OFF |

Bus 6 is kept stable. Individual musicians control how much of the returned MD/PA channel they hear from their own PreSonus bus.

**Safety:** Ch 8 must never enter Bus 6 because Ch 8 originates from PreSonus. Returning it creates a feedback loop.
